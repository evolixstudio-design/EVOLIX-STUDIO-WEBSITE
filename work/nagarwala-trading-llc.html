From No Website to a Quotation-Ready Digital Catalogue

A commercial catalogue website that helps buyers discover products and begin a quotation conversation.

01 // THE CHALLENGE

No Focused Digital Destination for Product Discovery

Nagarwala Trading LLC, a growing building materials supplier based in Dubai, UAE, lacked a dedicated digital footprint. Despite having a strong local network, their product discovery and quotation process relied entirely on manual interactions—sharing disjointed PDFs, making phone calls, and sending WhatsApp messages.

This lack of a centralized online presence severely hindered their ability to scale. Prospective B2B buyers could not independently browse their extensive catalogue of construction materials, hardware, and safety gear. The absence of a professional website also impacted their brand credibility when competing for larger, commercial supply contracts in the competitive UAE market.

02 // OUR APPROACH

Designing a Quotation-Led Conversion Path

Instead of building a static, brochure-style company profile, Evolix Studio recognized that Nagarwala needed a functional sales tool. Our approach was to design a quotation-led commercial catalogue. We focused on organizing their diverse product lines into an easily browsable digital format that prioritized user experience and searchability.

We engineered the user journey so that every product page served as a landing page for potential leads. By integrating clear call-to-action buttons for bulk quotations and direct WhatsApp inquiries, we ensured that the website actively facilitated sales conversations rather than passively displaying information.

03 // WHAT WE DELIVERED

Engineered Digital Assets

We delivered a comprehensive digital catalogue tailored for B2B material sourcing.

  • Responsive commercial landing page optimized for Dubai's B2B sector.
  • Structured product and category catalogue for building materials.
  • Streamlined quotation-request pathway and mobile-friendly contact actions.
  • Professional digital presentation designed to build trust with contractors.
04 // TECH STACK & RESULTS

Fast Delivery & Immediate Impact

The website was developed utilizing clean HTML/CSS and lightweight JavaScript to ensure ultra-fast loading speeds across the Middle East. The architecture was specifically optimized for local SEO, helping Nagarwala rank for material supply queries in Dubai.

Delivered within a strict 15-day timeline, the new digital catalogue immediately transformed how Nagarwala Trading conducts business. The sales team now leverages the platform as their primary presentation tool, resulting in a significant increase in inbound quotation requests and an elevated professional image.
