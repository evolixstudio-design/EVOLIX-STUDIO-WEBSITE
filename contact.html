Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about starting a project, onboarding timelines, deliverables, and confidentiality agreements with Evolix Studio.

01. How soon can we kick off a new project?

Following your initial discovery inquiry and requirements briefing, we organize a 30-minute scope alignment session. Upon agreement on milestones and technical architecture, project kickoffs typically commence within 24 to 48 hours.

02. What is the standard engagement workflow?

We operate in 4 agile phases: Strategy & Wireframing, High-Fidelity UI/UX & Prototyping, Production Engineering (clean code, Core Web Vitals optimization), and Launch QA followed by analytics and growth support.

03. Do you sign Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs)?

Absolutely. Client confidentiality, proprietary algorithms, brand assets, and business data are treated with the highest enterprise-level security. We are pleased to execute mutual NDAs prior to in-depth technical discussions.

04. What industries and client scales do you work with?

We partner with ambitious startups, scaling direct-to-consumer (D2C) brands, manufacturing distributors, B2B commercial suppliers, and technology companies worldwide requiring specialized web, branding, and custom software systems.

05. What deliverables do we receive upon project completion?

You receive 100% intellectual property ownership, complete source code repositories, design Figma source files, vector assets, API documentation, and comprehensive video walkthroughs for seamless internal handoff.

06. Do you offer post-launch maintenance & SLA support?

Yes. We provide dedicated monthly maintenance agreements covering security patches, Core Web Vitals performance tuning, server uptime monitoring, continuous SEO tracking, and feature expansion.
