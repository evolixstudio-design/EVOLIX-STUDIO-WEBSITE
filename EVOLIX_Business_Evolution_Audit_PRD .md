## 1. Executive Summary

The EVOLIX Business Evolution Audit is a short, mobile-first self-assessment designed for people who scan the QR code on the EVOLIX ₹100 promotional note.

The experience must not feel like a generic contact form or an agency sales page. It should feel like a quick business diagnostic. The visitor answers **7 simple questions**, enters basic business details, consents to receive relevant recommendations, and immediately receives an **EVOLIX Score /100**.

The result page then shows:

- Overall EVOLIX Score /100
- Score band and short interpretation
- Strongest area
- Biggest opportunity
- Second opportunity
- Three personalized next moves
- One CTA: **Get My Evolution Roadmap**

The CTA opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message containing the visitor's score. EVOLIX receives the lead details and all seven answers so the sales conversation can begin with context rather than a cold pitch.

### Campaign principle

> **Do not sell a website, software, branding or marketing first. Diagnose the business first.**

The audit is the bridge between the Purple Cow ₹100 note and EVOLIX's broader positioning as a digital solutions brand that identifies business bottlenecks and builds the right solution.

## 2. Product Goals

### Primary goals

1. Convert QR scans into qualified business leads.
2. Capture the visitor's WhatsApp number only after they have completed the audit.
3. Give instant value before asking for a sales conversation.
4. Identify the prospect's likely weak areas before EVOLIX contacts them.
5. Position EVOLIX as a business problem-solver, not a generic agency.

### Secondary goals

- Make the ₹100 campaign memorable and shareable.
- Create a repeatable entry point for offline events, networking, cold outreach and social media.
- Build first-party lead data around business pain points.
- Give the EVOLIX team a structured opening for WhatsApp follow-up.

### Non-goals

- This is not a scientific business valuation.
- This is not a financial or legal assessment.
- It should not attempt to diagnose every part of a company.
- It should not ask long or sensitive questions.
- It should not show multiple competing sales CTAs.

## 6. Scoring System - Final

### Maximum score

| Area | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Brand Clarity | 10 |
| Online Presence | 15 |
| Lead Generation | 15 |
| Sales Process | 15 |
| Customer Information | 15 |
| Operations | 15 |

| **TOTAL** | **100** |

### Formula

`EVOLIX Score = Q1 points + Q2 points + Q3 points + Q4 points + Q5 points + Q6 points + Q7 points`

No AI is required for the score. It should be deterministic and instant.

### Normalized area scores for visual comparison

To compare a 10-point question with a 15-point question fairly on the result page, normalize each answer:

- A = 100%
- B = approximately 67-70%
- C = approximately 33-40%
- D = 0%

The overall score remains the weighted score out of 100.

## 9. Personalized Recommendation Engine

The system should select the **three weakest normalized areas** and show one recommendation for each.

### Q1 - Brand Clarity recommendation

**Title:** `Make your value clear in seconds`

**Copy:**  
Clarify what you do, who you help and why a customer should choose you. Use the same positioning consistently across your website, social profiles, sales material and introductions.

---

### Q2 - Online Presence recommendation

**Title:** `Strengthen what customers find before they contact you`

**Copy:**  
Make sure your website, Google presence and social profiles are current, trustworthy and consistent. Every touchpoint should make the next action obvious.

---

### Q3 - Lead Generation recommendation

**Title:** `Build a more predictable enquiry engine`

**Copy:**  
Reduce dependence on random enquiries or referrals alone. Develop at least one repeatable channel that consistently brings the right prospects into your business.

---

### Q4 - Sales Process recommendation

**Title:** `Turn enquiries into a defined sales flow`

**Copy:**  
Create a clear process from enquiry to follow-up, quotation and payment so opportunities are not lost because of inconsistent handling.

---

### Q5 - Customer Information recommendation

**Title:** `Create one reliable source of customer information`

**Copy:**  
Bring customer, sales and follow-up information into a structured system so your team is not depending on scattered chats, notebooks or disconnected files.

---

### Q6 - Operations recommendation

**Title:** `Remove repetitive work from daily operations`

**Copy:**  
Identify the tasks your team repeats most often and systemise the highest-impact ones first. The goal is fewer manual steps, fewer errors and more visibility.

---

### Q7 - Scalability recommendation

**Title:** `Prepare your systems before growth creates pressure`

**Copy:**  
Document the processes that would break first if demand doubled. Strengthen those areas before adding more customers, staff or locations.

---

### Recommendation rules

1. Rank all seven areas from weakest to strongest using normalized answer level.
2. Display the lowest three.
3. If the visitor answers A on all seven questions, do not invent weaknesses. Show:
   - `Improve conversion intelligence`
   - `Connect systems and reporting`
   - `Explore advanced automation and scale`
4. Do not claim EVOLIX has inspected their business beyond the answers provided.
5. Use language such as `may`, `could`, `appears`, and `based on your answers` where appropriate.

## 14. Recommended UI / UX Direction

### Brand feel

The audit should feel like a premium EVOLIX product, not a questionnaire template.

**Recommended visual direction:**

- Ivory / warm off-white background
- Charcoal / near-black typography
- Restrained gold accent
- Large editorial numbers for score and progress
- Thin grid lines / subtle card borders
- Strong whitespace
- No stock photos
- No unnecessary illustrations
- No generic gradient-heavy AI aesthetic

### Mobile-first requirements

- Design for QR traffic first
- Large touch targets
- One answer card per option
- Sticky progress indicator
- Keep main CTA visible without covering content
- Avoid horizontal scrolling
- Result score visible above the fold

### Interaction rules

- Selecting an answer can enable the Continue button; do not auto-jump instantly unless animation is subtle
- Animate progress lightly, not theatrically
- Do not use confetti or game-like effects
- Score reveal can use a short count-up animation from 0 to final score
- Maintain browser Back safety so answers are not accidentally lost

## 15. Data, Privacy and Trust Requirements

- Clearly identify EVOLIX as the collector of submitted information.
- The consent checkbox must not be pre-checked.
- Only collect fields required for the stated follow-up.
- Do not expose submitted leads in client-side source code or public endpoints.
- Use HTTPS.
- Validate and sanitize all input server-side.
- Add rate limiting / bot protection appropriate to the existing website stack.
- Store consent timestamp with each lead.
- Provide a link to the site's privacy policy near the form if available.
- The score must be described as a quick business self-assessment, not an audited certification.

Suggested footer note:

`Your EVOLIX Score is a quick self-assessment based on the answers you provide. It is intended to highlight possible areas for improvement and is not a financial, legal or certified business evaluation.`

## 17. Suggested Technical Logic

### Front-end state

Keep these values during the audit session:

```text
answers.q1 = A/B/C/D
answers.q2 = A/B/C/D
answers.q3 = A/B/C/D
answers.q4 = A/B/C/D
answers.q5 = A/B/C/D
answers.q6 = A/B/C/D
answers.q7 = A/B/C/D
lead.name
lead.business_name
lead.whatsapp
lead.industry
lead.website_or_instagram
lead.consent
```

### Scoring pseudocode

```text
Q1 = { A:10, B:7, C:4, D:0 }
Q2-Q7 = { A:15, B:10, C:5, D:0 }

score = q1 + q2 + q3 + q4 + q5 + q6 + q7
```

### Result selection pseudocode

```text
1. Convert each answer to normalized strength:
   A = 100
   B = 67
   C = 33
   D = 0

2. Highest normalized value = strongest area.
3. Sort ascending = opportunity ranking.
4. Use business-impact tie priority for equal values.
5. Pull recommendations for the first 3 opportunities.
6. Determine score band from overall score.
7. Render result.
```

### Submission sequence

1. Validate all seven answers.
2. Validate lead form + consent.
3. Calculate score on the server or recalculate server-side before storage.
4. Save lead and answers.
5. Return score + band + insights.
6. Render result page.

Do not trust only a client-side score if the score is also saved in the database.

## 19. Acceptance Criteria

The feature is ready to launch only when all of the following pass:

- QR destination loads correctly on mobile.
- All 7 finalized questions appear exactly as approved.
- User cannot reveal a score without completing all 7 questions.
- Required lead fields are enforced.
- Website / Instagram remains optional.
- Consent checkbox is required and not pre-checked.
- Score always totals correctly out of 100.
- Score band matches the defined range.
- Strongest and weakest areas are calculated correctly.
- Exactly 3 personalized recommendations appear.
- Lead data is saved successfully before / with result reveal.
