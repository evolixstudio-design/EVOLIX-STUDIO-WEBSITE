What a Complete Brand Identity Includes

We don't just hand over a graphic and call it a day. A true brand identity is a systematic visual language designed to be consistent across every touchpoint. Our comprehensive branding packages include:

  • Logo & Mark Design: Primary, secondary, and responsive logo variations.
  • Color Palette: Strategic primary and accent colors with CMYK and HEX codes.
  • Typography Hierarchy: Hand-picked font pairings for headings, body text, and accents.
  • Brand Guidelines: A definitive rulebook detailing how to use (and not use) your brand assets.
  • Packaging & Mockups: High-end digital mockups showing your brand in the real world.
