Amazon A+ Content & Storefront Design

In the highly competitive Amazon marketplace, standard text descriptions are no longer enough to win the buy box. Evolix Studio specializes in designing premium Amazon A+ Content (formerly EBC) and complete Brand Storefronts that elevate your product's perceived value, educate the buyer, and directly increase conversion rates.

Why A+ Content Matters

A+ Content allows registered brands to replace basic product descriptions with rich text, high-quality images, and comparative charts. This visual storytelling does more than just look good; it proactively answers customer questions before they are asked, reducing negative reviews and product returns while significantly boosting your conversion rate.

Our Comprehensive Design Modules

We don't use generic templates. Every A+ module we create is custom-designed to fit your specific product's unique selling propositions (USPs). Our deliverables include:

  • High-End Infographics: Visually breaking down complex features, dimensions, or usage instructions.
  • Lifestyle Composites: Showing the product in action to help the customer visualize ownership.
  • Comparison Charts: Cross-selling your other products to keep customers within your brand ecosystem.
  • Brand Story Modules: Building trust by highlighting your company's history, mission, and quality standards.

Full Amazon Storefront Design

Your Amazon Storefront acts as your independent website within the Amazon ecosystem. We design multi-page storefronts that categorize your products intuitively, feature high-quality hero banners, and provide a distraction-free shopping environment away from your competitors' sponsored ads.
