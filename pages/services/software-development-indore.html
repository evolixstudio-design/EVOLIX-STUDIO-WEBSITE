Custom Software Development in Indore

Your business software should make the daily work easier to manage. Evolix Studio develops custom software in Indore for teams that need more than disconnected spreadsheets or a generic application. We design business applications around the way orders, customers, inventory and information move through your company, with a clear scope for what the system should improve.

Business software built around your workflow

A useful software project starts with understanding the current process. We map the people involved, the information they use and the points where work is repeated or delayed. From there, we can plan a CRM, internal portal, reporting dashboard or a larger business management system. The goal is a coherent workflow with clear ownership of each task and record.

CRM, ERP and operational dashboards

Custom CRM development can bring leads, customer history and follow-ups into one workspace. ERP software can connect quotations, inventory, orders and expenses where those functions belong together. Dashboards help teams read the information relevant to their role. Rather than adding every feature at once, we agree on the modules needed for an initial release and how they will work together.

Integrations and business automation

Copying the same information between tools creates avoidable work. We review opportunities to connect your website, forms, existing applications and third-party services using supported interfaces. Automation can help route inquiries, update records or prepare reports. Each integration is scoped around the data available, access permissions and how failures or duplicate records should be handled.

See the work behind the service

Our portfolio includes a custom ERP for Shieldmax Safety in Kuwait and a delivery CRM for Mom's Kitchen in Dubai. These projects show how software can be shaped around specific operational needs. Read the case studies to see the problem, the scope and the resulting system, then tell us which parts of your own workflow need attention.

A practical development and handover process

We move from requirements and interface planning to development, testing and release. Important checks include user permissions, validation, core workflows and the handling of missing or incorrect information. We also discuss hosting, backups, maintenance and future changes. If you already have software, we first review what works and whether an integration, a focused improvement or a new application is the right next step.
