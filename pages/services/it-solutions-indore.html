IT Solutions for Growing Businesses

Evolix Studio provides IT solutions in Indore for businesses that want their digital tools to work together. We bring website development, app development, custom software and business automation into one practical conversation. Whether you are starting with a website or improving a workflow across several systems, we help define what the business needs and how to approach it.

A clear plan for your digital systems

The first step is understanding the tools you already use and the work they support. We review how customers reach you, how the team manages information and where processes become difficult to track. That creates a basis for prioritizing the next improvement. A focused change to a website or workflow may be more useful than replacing every tool at once.

Websites and applications that support customers

Your website is often the first point of contact with the business. We design and develop responsive websites, product catalogs and ecommerce experiences with clear navigation and inquiry paths. For repeated tasks, customer portals, mobile applications or progressive web apps may provide a better experience. We review the requirements before recommending a platform or development approach.

Custom software for the work behind the website

Customer-facing experiences often depend on internal systems. Our custom software services cover CRM platforms, ERP modules, reporting dashboards and business tools. We can map how a new inquiry becomes a customer record, quotation or order, then define how information should move through the team. Access, validation and responsibility for updates are part of that planning.

Integration and business process automation

When supported interfaces are available, integrations can reduce repeated data entry between systems. We discuss the records that need to be shared, how often they change and what should happen when a connection fails. Automation is scoped to a real task, such as routing an inquiry or updating an order status, with people able to understand and manage the result.

Build in stages and plan for ongoing use

We agree on the priorities, the initial release and the checks needed before launch. Hosting, handover, updates and maintenance should be clear alongside development. Our team is based in Indore and works with businesses in India, UAE and Kuwait. Share your current tools and the main problem you want to solve, and we can propose a suitable next step.

We can review how website inquiries and campaign landing pages connect with your lead-handling process, including CRM options where appropriate.

Share your goals, current setup and priorities. Evolix Studio will help define a practical scope for your project.
