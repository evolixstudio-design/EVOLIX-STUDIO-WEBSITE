A Machinery Catalogue Website Delivered in 15 Days

A professional industrial catalogue website designed to improve credibility, product discovery and customer enquiries.

01 // THE CHALLENGE

Limited Online Visibility in the B2B Machinery Sector

Krishna Tools, a well-established industrial machinery supplier in Indore, Madhya Pradesh, faced a significant digital roadblock. Despite having a strong local reputation and an extensive inventory of commercial tools and heavy machinery, their online presence was practically non-existent. They had no central digital destination for presenting their technical product range to commercial B2B buyers.

Consequently, the sales team spent countless hours manually emailing PDF catalogues and answering basic technical specifications over the phone. They needed a professional, highly organized machinery catalogue website that could act as a 24/7 digital showroom, improve local search credibility, and streamline the customer enquiry process—and they needed it launched before an upcoming industry expo.

02 // OUR APPROACH

Structured Industrial Architecture & Rapid Deployment

Understanding the strict 15-day deadline, Evolix Studio adopted a highly agile web development approach. We immediately prioritized information architecture, structuring the user experience around clear product categories, detailed technical specifications, and highly visible B2B enquiry actions.

Unlike retail e-commerce, B2B machinery buyers don't usually 'add to cart.' They request custom quotations. Therefore, our design focused entirely on the lead generation funnel. We stripped away unnecessary design fluff in favor of a clean, industrial aesthetic that communicated reliability, ensuring that every product page had a clear, low-friction pathway for users to request technical data or a price quote.

03 // WHAT WE DELIVERED

A Comprehensive B2B Catalogue Platform

We delivered a high-performance industrial website built precisely for lead generation.

  • Responsive industrial website optimized for both desktop and mobile field-use.
  • Deeply categorized machinery & tool catalogue with search functionality.
  • Technical product presentation layouts for complex machine specifications.
  • Automated quotation & direct WhatsApp enquiry pathways.
04 // TECH STACK & RESULTS

Performance-First Engineering

To meet the rapid delivery timeline without compromising quality, the platform was developed using a modern, lightweight frontend stack optimized for technical SEO. Clean HTML5 semantics, optimized CSS, and fast-loading image compression ensured the site passed Core Web Vitals, crucial for ranking in Google search results.

Delivered exactly within the 15-day window, the website immediately provided Krishna Tools with a professional digital footprint. The sales team now directs prospects to the online catalog, dramatically reducing manual administrative work and increasing the volume of qualified inbound B2B leads.
