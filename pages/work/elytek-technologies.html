01 // THE CHALLENGE

A Standard Catalogue Could Not Represent Spatial Innovation

Elytek Technologies operates in a highly visual and technical field, delivering integrated display, interactive, lighting, and technology solutions. Their existing digital presence was failing to communicate the true scale, precision, and visual character of the company's enterprise-grade work. They were perceived as just another supplier rather than an architectural technology partner.

A conventional catalogue-style layout would not adequately reflect a business working with immersive LED displays, architectural illumination, interactive kiosks, and network infrastructure. The primary goal was to create a digital platform that visitors could truly experience—not merely read. Elytek needed a website that served as a direct demonstration of their spatial capabilities.

02 // OUR APPROACH

Engineered Visual Impact & Cinematic 3D Surfaces

Evolix Studio developed the website around the core concept of “engineered visual impact.” We bypassed standard web templates and opted for dark cinematic surfaces, technical HUD-inspired interface details, and luminous cyan-blue accents that directly echo Elytek's actual hardware interfaces in the field.

Crucially, we integrated custom 3D motion sequences. These are not decorative additions; they act as interactive visual demonstrations of display structures, illumination patterns, and signal interactions. By incorporating these WebGL elements, the website itself became a symbol of what Elytek builds in physical spaces.

03 // WHAT WE DELIVERED

A Comprehensive 3D Digital Platform

We executed a complete overhaul of Elytek's digital presence, delivering a high-performance, visually striking platform.

  • Custom technology-led website interface with dark mode aesthetics.
  • Dynamic homepage featuring 3D product and system animations.
  • Dedicated interactive showcases for architectural lighting and LED systems.
  • Responsive mobile architecture ensuring performance despite heavy visual assets.
04 // TECH STACK & RESULTS

WebGL and Frontend Performance

To achieve the fluid, cinematic experience required, we utilized Three.js and WebGL for the 3D rendering pipeline directly in the browser. The core site architecture was built using a lightweight static site generator with highly optimized CSS/JS to ensure rapid load times even on mobile connections.

The launch resulted in a significant elevation of Elytek's brand perception, leading to an increase in high-ticket enterprise inquiries and successfully positioning them as an industry leader rather than a commodity supplier.
