01 // PROJECT OVERVIEW

Apna Galla

Apna Galla is an Evolix product for retail shops. Its public website introduces billing, inventory, khata, purchases, expenses and everyday shop management workflows.

02 // PUBLIC EXPERIENCE

What the screenshots show

The public site includes feature information and a browser-based sample-shop demonstration. The screenshots show the product website, not a retailer’s private account or financial records.

03 // PROJECT AVAILABILITY

Website & product status

The web product is available at apnagalla.in. The owner has described a mobile app as upcoming. Retailers using Apna Galla can connect their shop with Apna Mart for customer ordering; the two products are presented separately here.
