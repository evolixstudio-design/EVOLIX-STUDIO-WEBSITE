Custom Delivery CRM Dubai, UAE 1 month duration

One Custom CRM for Orders, Delivery and Finance

A cloud-kitchen CRM connecting orders, delivery, billing, finance and communication in one operational workspace.

01 // THE CHALLENGE

Fragmented Operations in a High-Volume Cloud Kitchen

Mom's Kitchen, a rapidly scaling cloud kitchen in Dubai, was struggling with the complexities of managing high-volume food delivery during peak rush hours. Their operational ecosystem was heavily fragmented: incoming orders, customer communications, rider dispatch, and daily accounting were all being handled across separate, disconnected platforms.

This lack of centralization created a chaotic kitchen environment. Miscommunications led to delayed orders, riders were often left waiting without clear dispatch instructions, and daily financial reconciliation became an administrative nightmare. They needed a single source of truth to unify their entire operation.

02 // OUR APPROACH

Engineering an End-to-End Kitchen Workflow

We didn't just build software; we engineered a digital assembly line. Evolix Studio designed the CRM specifically around the high-pressure daily rhythm of a Dubai cloud kitchen. Our approach was to create a seamless, linear data flow from the exact moment an order is received to the moment the delivery rider hands it to the customer.

We focused heavily on the user interface for the kitchen staff, ensuring critical information like order modifications, dietary restrictions, and rider ETAs were highly visible. By automating the hand-off between the kitchen and the dispatch team, we eliminated the need for verbal confirmations and reduced the cognitive load on the staff.

03 // WHAT WE DELIVERED

Integrated Kitchen Suite

We deployed a bespoke cloud-kitchen CRM that completely unified their operational workspace.

  • Live operations dashboard for multi-channel order management.
  • Automated delivery and rider assignment workflow.
  • Integrated billing with one-click printable receipts.
  • Centralized finance, expense ledger, and AED-based currency handling.
04 // TECH STACK & RESULTS

Speed and Reliability

To ensure zero downtime during peak service hours, the CRM was built using a high-performance React.js frontend paired with a scalable Node.js backend. Real-time order updates were managed via WebSockets, ensuring the kitchen display screens updated instantly without manual refreshing.

Following implementation, Mom's Kitchen reduced their average order processing time by 35% and virtually eliminated dispatch errors, allowing them to comfortably handle double their previous order volume.
