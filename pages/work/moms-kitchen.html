One Custom CRM for Orders, Delivery and Finance

A cloud-kitchen CRM connecting orders, delivery, billing, finance and communication in one operational workspace.

01 // THE CHALLENGE

Fragmented Operations

Separate order, customer, delivery, billing and accounting processes created avoidable complexity and increased the risk of mistakes across peak rush hours.

02 // EVOLIX APPROACH

End-to-End Kitchen Workflow

Evolix designed the system around the daily rhythm of a Dubai cloud kitchen, connecting the flow from order intake to billing, rider assignment, delivery and finance.

03 // DELIVERABLES

Integrated Kitchen Suite

  • Live operations dashboard
  • Multi-channel order management
  • Delivery and rider workflow
  • Billing and printable receipts
  • Finance & expense ledger
  • Customer communication workspace
  • AED-based multi-currency interface
