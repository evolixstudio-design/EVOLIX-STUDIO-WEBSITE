What is Amazon A+ Content & Why Your Brand Needs It

By Evolix Studio • 4 min read • E-Commerce

If you are selling products on Amazon, you already know how fiercely competitive the marketplace is. Having a great product is no longer enough; you need to visually convince the customer before they scroll down to your competitors' sponsored ads. This is where Amazon A+ Content (formerly Enhanced Brand Content) becomes your most powerful sales tool.

Understanding A+ Content

Available to brand-registered sellers, A+ Content allows you to replace the standard, text-heavy product description with rich, custom-designed graphic modules. Instead of a boring block of text, you can feature high-definition lifestyle photography, detailed infographics, comparison charts, and branded banners.

Why is A+ Content Critical for Conversion?

  • Overcoming the Skim-Reader: The modern consumer rarely reads paragraphs of text. A+ Content allows you to communicate your product's unique selling points visually and instantly.
  • Answering Unasked Questions: High-quality infographics can detail dimensions, ingredients, or usage instructions, immediately resolving doubts that cause cart abandonment.
  • Brand Storytelling: Amazon can feel anonymous. A+ Content gives you the canvas to tell your brand's story, building trust and authority that leads to repeat purchases.
  • Reduced Return Rates: By setting accurate visual expectations and explaining exact product specifications, customers know exactly what they are buying, leading to fewer negative reviews and returns.
