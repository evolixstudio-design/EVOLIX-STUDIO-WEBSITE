# Krishna Tools — A Machinery Catalogue Website Delivered in 15 Days

**Service:** Website Development  
**Location:** Indore, Madhya Pradesh  
**Duration:** 15 days

## Overview

Evolix created a professional machinery catalogue website that improves Krishna Tools' digital credibility and gives customers a clearer way to discover products and make enquiries.

## Challenge

The business had limited online visibility and no strong digital destination for presenting its tools, machinery and technical product range.

## Evolix approach

We structured the website around commercial product discovery: clear categories, visual product presentation, technical information and visible enquiry actions. Delivery speed was important, so the project was completed within the committed 15-day window.

## What we delivered

- Responsive industrial website
- Machinery and tool catalogue
- Product/category navigation
- Technical product presentation
- Quotation and enquiry pathway
- Mobile-first contact actions

## Outcome

Krishna Tools gained a credible online presence, a simpler product-discovery experience and a direct channel for customer enquiries.
