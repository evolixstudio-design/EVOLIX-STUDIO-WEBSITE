# Nut Delicacy — Turning a Product Idea Into a Launch-Ready Brand

**Service:** Brand Launch, Website & Organic Social Media  
**Location:** Surat, Gujarat  
**Duration:** Launch engagement

## Overview

Nut Delicacy began as a product idea. Evolix helped shape it into NJOY by Nut Delicacy—a complete consumer brand with identity direction, packaging presentation, product imagery, an interactive commerce experience and an organic launch presence.

## Challenge

The founder needed every customer-facing part of the brand to work together. The identity, product presentation, website, launch story and social content had to feel consistent from the first public impression.

## Evolix approach

We treated the project as one connected brand experience. The website was designed around discovery, rich product presentation, curated combinations and WhatsApp-assisted ordering, while launch content introduced the range with a consistent premium visual system.

## What we delivered

- Brand and visual direction
- Packaging and product-presentation support
- Product photography and campaign visuals
- Interactive website experience
- Product collection and combo presentation
- WhatsApp-assisted ordering pathway
- Organic launch strategy and social-media content

## Key experience features

- Story-led product experience
- Product-detail and label inspection
- Curated combos and tasting bag
- Launch offer interaction
- WhatsApp concierge ordering
- Mobile-friendly product discovery

## Outcome

Evolix helped take Nut Delicacy from an early idea to a coherent launched brand with the essential digital touchpoints needed to present and sell its products professionally. Do not claim numerical sales or reach without verified analytics.
