# Shieldmax Safety — ERP Built Around a Real Safety-Supply Workflow

**Service:** Custom ERP Software  
**Location:** Kuwait  
**Duration:** 1 month

## Overview

Shieldmax Safety needed more than a standard business application. Evolix designed a custom ERP around the way its team handles quotations, delivery orders, expenses and operational records.

## Challenge

Critical commercial documents and expenses were difficult to manage consistently. A generic off-the-shelf product would have forced the team to change its process instead of supporting it.

## Evolix approach

We mapped the working flow first, then built a focused system in which connected records move from quotation to delivery and expense tracking. The interface was structured for everyday use, clear status visibility and fewer disconnected records.

## What we delivered

- Central business dashboard
- Custom quotation workflow
- Delivery-order management
- Expense recording and visibility
- Connected operational records
- Role-aware, practical interface

## Outcome

Shieldmax gained one organised system aligned with its own workflow, making its core commercial and operational processes easier to manage.
