# Mom's Kitchen — One Custom CRM for Orders, Delivery and Finance

**Service:** Custom Delivery CRM  
**Location:** Dubai, UAE  
**Duration:** 1 month

## Overview

Evolix built a focused cloud-kitchen CRM that brings orders, delivery operations, billing, finance and communication into one coordinated system.

## Challenge

Managing incoming orders, customer details, delivery status, billing and accounts across separate processes created avoidable complexity and increased the risk of mistakes as order volume grew.

## Evolix approach

We designed the system around the daily rhythm of a Dubai cloud kitchen. Each module supports a connected operational flow—from receiving an order to billing, rider assignment, delivery tracking and financial visibility.

## What we delivered

- Live operations dashboard
- Multi-channel order management
- Delivery and rider workflow
- Fast billing and printable bills
- Finance ledger for income and expenses
- Customer communication workspace
- AED-based financial interface

## Key features

- Orders from website, WhatsApp and delivery channels
- Status flow from new to delivered
- Rider assignment
- VAT, discount and delivery-charge handling
- Income, expense, outstanding and profit visibility
- Menu-based billing

## Outcome

Mom's Kitchen gained one systematic workspace for key delivery operations, helping the team organise more orders with clearer control and fewer avoidable mistakes. Values shown inside screenshots are interface/demo data, not published performance claims.
