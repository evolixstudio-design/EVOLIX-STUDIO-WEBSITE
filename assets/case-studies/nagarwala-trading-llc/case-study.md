# Nagarwala Trading LLC — From No Website to a Quotation-Ready Digital Catalogue

**Service:** Website Development  
**Location:** Dubai, UAE  
**Duration:** 15 days

## Overview

Evolix created a professional commercial website that allows buyers to explore Nagarwala Trading's product catalogue and move directly toward requesting a quotation.

## Challenge

The business had no website or focused landing page. Product discovery and quotation conversations depended on manual sharing, limiting credibility and convenience for new buyers.

## Evolix approach

We combined a clear landing experience, browsable catalogue and quotation-led conversion path. The website was designed as a sales-support tool rather than a static company profile.

## What we delivered

- Responsive commercial landing page
- Product/category catalogue
- Product discovery flow
- Quotation-request pathway
- Mobile-friendly contact actions
- Professional digital presentation

## Outcome

Nagarwala Trading gained a credible online destination where prospective customers can understand its offering and begin a quotation conversation more easily.
