# Al Marjan Collection — Launching a Fashion Brand Through Organic Social Media

**Service:** Organic Social Media Marketing  
**Location:** Neemuch, Madhya Pradesh  
**Duration:** 3–4 month engagement

## Overview

Al Marjan Collection started without an established digital presence. Evolix built the foundation—from profile positioning and launch communication to consistent fashion-led content and an enquiry-focused organic funnel.

## Challenge

The brand needed to introduce itself online, communicate a premium identity and consistently present its Rida, jodi and women's-wear collection without relying on paid promotion.

## Evolix approach

We created a soft, elegant visual language around the brand identity, organised the Instagram profile, planned launch content and developed product-led posts and reels designed for organic discovery.

## What we delivered

- Instagram profile positioning and optimisation
- Organic launch strategy
- Content planning and publishing direction
- Branded posts, carousels and reels
- Product presentation and visual consistency
- Captions, calls to action and enquiry funnel

## Verified organic proof

- 7.3K Instagram views in a 30-day period
- A featured product creative reached 1.9K views

These figures come from the supplied Instagram screenshots and should be displayed with the label `Organic Instagram performance`.

## Outcome

Al Marjan moved from no digital presence to a consistent, recognisable profile capable of presenting products, attracting discovery and supporting customer enquiries organically.
