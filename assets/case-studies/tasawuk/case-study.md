# Tasawuk — Consistent Industrial Marketing, Supported by Paid Reach

**Service:** Social Media Management & Paid Advertising  
**Location:** Kuwait  
**Duration:** 3–4 month engagement

## Overview

Tasawuk needed a dependable Instagram system for an industrial and tools audience. Evolix combined profile optimisation, consistent organic content and paid advertising so the client could focus on handling and converting incoming opportunities.

## Challenge

Maintaining regular reels and posts was difficult for the internal team. The profile needed clearer positioning, consistent product communication and wider relevant discovery.

## Evolix approach

We planned and maintained the content pipeline, created industrial product-led reels and posts, optimised the profile and supported distribution with paid advertising. Organic management and ad-supported reach must be described separately.

## What we delivered

- Instagram profile optimisation
- Organic content strategy and management
- Industrial product reels and posts
- Captions and conversion-focused calls to action
- Paid advertising setup and campaign support
- Performance monitoring and content refinement

## Verified combined performance

- 111,975 Instagram views over a supplied 30-day reporting period
- 98.1% of views came from non-followers
- 35,557 viewers shown in the supplied Insights data

Label these figures `Organic content + paid distribution` and do not represent them as fully organic results.

## Outcome

Tasawuk gained a maintained social presence and broader discovery system, allowing the client to spend less time managing Instagram and more time handling enquiries and conversions.
